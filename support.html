Support

Apple TV

Slide your thumb up or down on the Siri Remote touch surface to move the paddle. Hold still to stop. Lift your thumb and touch down again anywhere to continue moving. Back pauses the match. Compatible controllers use the left stick or directional pad.

Settings has touch sensitivity, button movement, music and effects controls, reduced motion, trails, and result effects. After an interruption, choose Resume. Continue Match restores a saved match from the main menu.

Apple Watch

Tap Start and turn the Digital Crown to move the paddle. One miss ends the run. Tap Pause to keep your run for later, or choose Play Again at the result screen. Your best score stays on the watch.
