Privacy policy

DUEL CPU for Apple TV and DUEL CPU Survival for Apple Watch do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data during gameplay.

Information stored on your device

Apple TV stores your settings, records, presets, and saved match on the device. Apple Watch stores your best score and saved Survival run on the watch. Both games work offline and use no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Apple and support

Apple may separately process purchases or diagnostics under its own privacy policies. If you email support, the developer uses the information you choose to share to respond to your request.
