DUEL

Privacy policy

DUEL on Apple TV, iPhone, and Apple Watch does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data during gameplay.

Information stored on your device

Each edition stores its settings, records, and saved match on the device where you play. Apple TV also stores your custom presets. The games work offline and use no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Delete DUEL from your device to remove its local game data.

Apple and support

Apple may separately process purchases or diagnostics under its own privacy policies. If you email support, the developer uses the information you choose to share to respond to your request.

Contact shoshanadean@gmail.com with privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence.
